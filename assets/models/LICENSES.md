[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain dedication).
You can use, modify and ship them in any project, including commercial ones, without attribution.

## Contributed models

Contributed models must be **CC0-1.0** or **CC-BY-4.0**. Add a row below for every contributed file,
with its source and author. CC-BY models must credit the author here, and anyone redistributing
the kit must keep that credit.
